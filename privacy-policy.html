ChroniVue

Privacy Policy · Effective date: November 5, 2026

Summary

ChroniVue is a camera app that adds the date, time and location to your photos and videos, and includes a document scanner and QR code reader. ChroniVue has no user accounts and no advertising, and it does not sell your data. Your photos, videos and scanned documents stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Photos, videos, scanned pages and PDF files you create are saved only on your device (in DCIM and Download folders you can see in your gallery or file manager).
  • Your settings, tags, project names, brand name, logo, custom text and the last known location and address are stored on your device so the app opens faster.
  • For PDF reports, the time, location, address, tag and project of each photo you take are stored inside the app on your device.
  • If you turn on the travel line on the map, your route of the last 12 hours is stored on your device and deleted when you turn the option off.
  • QR codes are created and read on your device. Compass readings come from your phone's sensors and are not saved or sent.

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera: to take photos, record videos, scan documents and read QR codes.
  • Microphone: only to record sound while you record a video.
  • Location: to print coordinates, address, altitude and speed on your photos and videos. Location is used only while the app is open, and while a video you started keeps recording. It is provided by your phone's location service (Google Play services).
  • Notifications and foreground service: only to show recording controls while a video records.

You can turn off any permission at any time in your phone settings.

Information sent to other services

Some optional features need the internet. In these cases, only the information described below is sent:

  • Weather (when turned on): your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get the current temperature and conditions.
  • Map (when turned on): map pictures are downloaded from OpenStreetMap, which can see the area around you (about 300 meters at the default zoom). Downloaded map pictures are kept on your device to save data.
  • Address: the address is found by your phone's built-in geocoder service, which sends your coordinates to its provider (on most phones, Google).
  • Location service: your position is provided by Google Play services, which handles location data under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Text recognition (searchable PDF): text in your scans is read on your device by Google ML Kit. Your images and text are not sent anywhere. ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information, such as device model, app version, performance metrics, error codes and a per-installation identifier that does not identify you. This information is encrypted in transit. See ML Kit Terms.

Like any internet request, these services can also see your device's IP address. They have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not have servers that receive your photos, videos, documents or location.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your information.

Important to know

The date, location, address, QR code and map are printed on the picture itself. Anyone you share the picture with can see this information.

Removing your data

Clear the app data or uninstall ChroniVue to remove your settings, logo, reports data and saved location. Your photos, videos and PDF files remain in your device storage until you delete them.

Children

ChroniVue is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
